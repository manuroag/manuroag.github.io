My Master’s thesis (see also projects) focused on enhancing the KCD method’s predictive capabilities for protein Circular Dichroism (CD) spectra (Fig. 1). By designing and training deep Bayesian Neural Networks (BNNs), I achieved a 40% improvement in prediction accuracy compared to the original model. This work not only strengthened my expertise in PyTorch, data-driven modeling, and scientific computing but also reinforced my ability to translate theoretical concepts into practical tools, a skill I aim to further develop in my PhD.

Fig. 1. KCD-AI workflow. Four Bayesian Neural Networks were integrated into the KCD model to increase the accuracy in CD spectra predictions.

I thrive in collaborative environments and am eager to contribute my background in statistical physics and machine learning to a research group tackling challenges in Computational Biophysics, Protein Science, Molecular Dynamics, Force Fields, AI-driven Scientific Discovery, or Drug and Materials Discovery.
